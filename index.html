The return path is the part most products skip
A parent responds in one tap — we're seeing this at home too / this feels different at home / I'd like to talk — plus an optional line capped at 240 characters. The cap is the feature: it's what stops the channel becoming email.
Parked, deliberately · Aug 2026

Loop has no child-facing surface, and no child data path at all.

A screen where a child taps how their day went is personal information collected directly from a child under 13. That pulls Loop into COPPA proper — verifiable parental consent before collection, a child-specific notice, retention limits, and a parental right to review and delete.

It also splits by tier. A school can consent on parents' behalf for genuinely educational use. A direct Loop Family subscriber has no school in the chain, so the identical screen would sit on two different legal bases depending on who paid. That's a question for counsel, not for first principles.

What it costs, and the workaround
Parking it costs Loop exactly one thing: the child's voice — the line that makes a parent stop scrolling. So the voice is relayed by the parent instead. Same sentence on the card, collected from an adult account holder, none of the above applies.

The designed student experience — My Focus, three stars, never a score — is kept in parked/student.html as a record. It is not linked from the product and not part of the build.

The one thing that crosses over

A child's Boggie follows them home.

Showdown's characters are the only piece of the classroom's personality that belongs in a parent app. Everything else Loop shows a parent is interpretation; the Boggie is the child's own. It gives a parent something to ask about by name — and it gives the child a reason to open their side of Loop at all.

The framework

The Whole Learner

Six dimensions. Nobody takes a 60-question assessment — the picture emerges from signals that were already being generated. Adults see The Whole Learner; the child sees the same six as My Growth.

Showdown signal → dimension

A signal is evidence, not a score. Each one fans into dimensions with a weight.

Engine rules
  • Rolling 21-day window, recency-weighted (≈9-day half-life)
  • Source trust: school 1.0 · home 0.85 · child 0.7
  • 3+ signals before a dimension says anything — otherwise "getting to know"
  • A direction needs 2+ events pointing the same way. One good day is not a trend
  • Parents never see a number. Plain language only
  • The child never sees a level, trend, or score at all
One gap worth naming

None of the eight Showdown signals feeds 🔎 Curiosity. As specced, Curiosity can only ever be a home-and-child dimension — a connected family would see five dimensions lit by school and one that isn't. Either Showdown captures something like Wondering (a student asking their own question, choosing to go deeper), or Curiosity gets labeled home-sourced on purpose.
